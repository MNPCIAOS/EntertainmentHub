QUICK ANSWERS

Questions before contacting us?

Do I need to create an account?

No. Anyone can use the contact and feedback form, whether registered or not.

Can I request a movie?

Yes. Choose “Movie / series request” and tell us the title and any useful details you know.

Can I recommend a movie?

Absolutely. Choose “Movie / series recommendation” and tell us why you recommend it.

Can I report a broken video or poster?

Yes. Choose “Report a problem” and include the movie title plus a short description of the problem.
